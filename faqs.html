Frequently asked questions

Cannot find an answer? Get in touch with our team.

Is QuickVolunteer free?

Yes. It is free for everyone who asks for help and everyone who gives help.

Can I ask for help and volunteer with the same account?

Yes. One account lets you ask for help, give help, or both. You can change this in your profile at any time.

How long does a task take?

Every task takes between 15 and 60 minutes. Tasks can be in person or remote, by phone or online.

How are members verified?

Our administrators check every new account before it can post or accept tasks.

How are my hours recorded?

When a task is confirmed as completed, its duration is added to your hours and activity history.

What if something goes wrong?

Use Report a concern on any task or profile. An administrator reviews every report and can suspend an account. If someone is in danger, call 999.

Is my address shared?

No. Other members only see your neighbourhood. Your exact address is shared only with the volunteer you accept.
