Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026. This is a summary written for the student project prototype.

What we collect

Your name, email address, postcode, the way you take part, your skills and availability, the tasks you post or accept, reviews, and hours.

Why we use it

To verify accounts, match tasks to the right volunteers, record completed hours, and keep the community safe.

Who can see it

Other members see your first name, initial, neighbourhood, reviews and skills. Your exact address is shared only with a volunteer you accept. Administrators can see account details when verifying accounts or reviewing a concern.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by contacting us.
